quickmt-en-vi (CTranslate2, int8)

This is a modified copy of the model "quickmt/quickmt-en-vi" by quickmt
(https://huggingface.co/quickmt/quickmt-en-vi), licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: the Linear and Embeddings weights in model.bin were converted from float16 to int8,
with one scale per row (the scheme CTranslate2 uses for int8). Vocabulary, SentencePiece and
config files are unchanged. Translations are identical to running the original model with
CTranslate2 compute_type="int8".
